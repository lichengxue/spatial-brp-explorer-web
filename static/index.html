Regional SPR weighting

What does this change?

Applies to Reg-Move, displayed regional SPRs, and Yield-SPR constraints. Other BRP fishing rates and unconstrained yield/MSY objectives keep their own definitions. The same origin weights are used in fished and unfished calculations. Survey and catch shares are assigned to origins by birth region; with mixing they are proxies, not observed origin proportions. Alternative mixes define regional spawning-output indices and leave the recruitment model and its inputs unchanged.

Seasonal movement

Arrows show the percentage of surviving fish moving at each season end. Other fish stay in their current region. Natal homing replaces the second transfer with complete return to the birth region. Spawning occurs at 0.625 years, before the third transfer.

Selectivity, weight & maturity

Edit each age or import a CSV. Selectivity and maturity range from 0 to 1; weight is in kg. Each fleet must select some fish and each biology type must have positive spawning output at some age. Survey gear uses the model's standard survey selectivity.
